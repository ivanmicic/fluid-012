First line of defence

Quick self-help — fix it in 2 minutes

Before you call, check the 4 most common reasons the heating will not run. In many cases a visit is not needed.

1 Boiler pressure has dropped (water has leaked / gauge in the red)

Look at the gauge on the boiler (an analogue needle or a digital display). If the pressure is below 1.0 bar (it often flashes or sits in the red zone), the system does not have enough water.

Quick fix:
  1. Find the blue or black filling valve on the underside of the boiler.
  2. Open it slowly until the needle or display shows between 1.2 and 1.5 bar.
  3. Close the valve firmly after filling.
2 The boiler does not respond to the room thermostat at all

This is the most common problem at the start of the heating season. The thermostat looks as if it works, but it does not have enough power to send a signal to the boiler.

Quick fix: Replace the batteries in the room thermostat (even if the screen is lit). Always use new alkaline batteries (AA or AAA). In 80% of these cases the boiler starts straight away.
3 A fault is showing (codes F22, F28, or a red light)

The lockout comes from a brief drop in gas pressure, a power cut, or air in the system.

Quick fix: Press and hold the Reset button (or the button with a flame or a crossed-out lightning symbol) for 3 seconds. Do not reset the boiler more than twice in a row. If the fault returns, it needs a service visit.
4 Radiators are cold at the top and hot at the bottom (or they gurgle)

Air has collected in the radiator and stops hot water circulating in the upper part.

Quick fix: Use a radiator bleed key. Open the valve on the side of the radiator until the air is gone and a thin stream of water comes out. Then check the boiler pressure and top it up if it has dropped.

What does a full check and regular service include?

  • 1. Burner and chamber Chemical and mechanical cleaning of the primary heat exchanger and the burner.
  • 2. Expansion vessel Pressure checked and topped up.
  • 3. Electronics and sensors Safety valves, probes and thermostat control.
  • 4. Combustion analysis Gas and air ratio set for steady running and lower use.
